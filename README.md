My dear data project is based on my anime watching experience as anime Is something I hold dearly to me as someone who does art and animation in my free time. 

I've collected data about individual episodes I've watched since the start of October and have recorded information such as the title, episode number, genre, duration, personal ranking, feelings during the episode, memorability and if I've watched it before. From this I aim to find out my watching habits and discover what types of anime I watch the most and the similarities/ differences between what I watch.
